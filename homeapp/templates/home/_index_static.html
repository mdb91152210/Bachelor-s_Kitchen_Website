// Our Story

Built for the
independent cook.

Bachelor's Kitchen started as a personal recipe notebook — scribbled down during college hostel days in Chennai, after craving a good dosa and realising there was no useful resource online.

We've turned it into a platform for anyone who wants to cook genuine South Indian food: not the simplified, fusion, or "easy weeknight" approximation — the real thing, made achievable with clear guidance and smart tools.

Every recipe is tested, every step timed, and every ingredient verified by home cooks who've made these dishes dozens of times.

🎯
Authenticity first

We don't simplify recipes to make them look easier. We break down complexity into clear steps so you can actually achieve the real result.

⏱️
Precision cooking

Built-in timers for every time-critical step, because "cook until done" isn't good enough when fermentation time determines dosa quality.

🤝
Community driven

Real reviews from real home cooks who've made these dishes in real kitchens. No sponsored content, no paid placements.

"Great South Indian food is never about the complexity of the spice. It is about patience, the right sequence, and ingredients that are still alive."
— Bachelor's Kitchen
